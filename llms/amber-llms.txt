# Amber Bio

> Amber Bio is a San Francisco-based biotechnology company founded in 2021, pioneering multi-kilobase RNA writing as a new gene-editing modality. The platform is designed to correct thousands of diverse mutations across a patient population with a single product, rather than requiring an independent therapy per mutation. Amber Bio is a pre-clinical therapeutics company and publishes no public API, developer portal, or SDK surface.
